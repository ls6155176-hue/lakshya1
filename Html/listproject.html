Lion

A lion is a large, powerful cat native to parts of Africa and Asia, known for being the only big cat to live in social groups called prides. Male lions are distinguished by their manes, while both males and females hunt for prey like wildebeest and antelope. Lions face threats from habitat loss and conflict with humans, with African lion populations now classified as "vulnerable".

Cow

A cow is a domesticated large herbivorous mammal commonly raised for its milk, meat, and leather. Cows are social animals that live in herds and are known for their gentle nature. They have a complex digestive system with four stomach compartments, allowing them to efficiently process fibrous plant material. Cows play a significant role in agriculture worldwide, providing essential products for human consumption

Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth, known for their intelligence, strong social bonds, and distinctive features such as long trunks and large ears. They are herbivores, feeding on a variety of vegetation including grasses, leaves, and fruits. Elephants play a crucial role in their ecosystems by shaping habitats and dispersing seeds. However, they face threats from habitat loss and poaching for their ivory tusks, leading to conservation efforts to protect these majestic creatures.

Dog

Dogs are domesticated mammals and one of the most popular pets worldwide, known for their loyalty, companionship, and diverse breeds. They have been bred for various purposes, including herding, hunting, and companionship. Dogs are highly social animals that thrive on interaction with humans and other dogs. They possess keen senses, particularly their sense of smell, which makes them valuable in roles such as search and rescue, therapy, and law enforcement. Proper care, training, and socialization are essential for a dog's well-being and happiness.
