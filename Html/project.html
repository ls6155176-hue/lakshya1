Nature is the most beautiful and precious gift of God to all living beings. It surrounds us with trees, mountains, rivers, and animals that make the Earth full of life. The fresh air, bright sunlight, and soothing sounds of birds bring peace and joy to our hearts. Nature provides us with everything we need — food, water, shelter, and oxygen. It teaches us balance and harmony, showing how every creature has a role to play. Protecting nature is our duty because a healthy environment means a healthy life. When we care for nature, it cares for us in return.
